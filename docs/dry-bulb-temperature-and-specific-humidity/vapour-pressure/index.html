    Input calculation

    Use the supplied dry bulb temperature and find W from the second property.

    W = q / (1 − q)

    Formula for vapour pressure

    The portion of the total air pressure contributed by water vapour. Also spelled vapor pressure.

    Pv = P W / (0.622 + W)

    Symbols

    q
    Specific humidity (SH), in kg water/kg moist air.
    W
    Intermediate water-vapour mass per kilogram of dry air, obtained from q/(1 − q) when known.
    Pv
    Water vapour partial pressure (PV), in kPa.
    P
    Total atmospheric pressure (PT), in kPa.

    Quantity definitions and calculation range

    Worked example

    Dry Bulb Temperature 27 °C · Specific Humidity 0.01 kg water/kg moist air · height 0 m
