    Input calculation

    Find the dry bulb temperature by matching the supplied enthalpy or specific volume along the constant relative humidity curve.

    Pv = (RH / 100) × Psat(Tdb)
    W = 0.622 × Pv / (P − Pv)
    v = 0.2871 (Tdb + 273.15)(1 + W/0.622) / P

    Formula for wet bulb temperature

    The temperature approached when water evaporates into air adiabatically until the air reaches saturation.

    h(Tdb, W) + (Ws(Twb) − W) hf(Twb) = h(Twb, Ws(Twb))

    Symbols

    Tdb
    Dry bulb temperature (DB), in °C.
    Twb
    Wet bulb temperature (WB), in °C.
    W
    Intermediate water-vapour mass per kilogram of dry air, obtained from q/(1 − q) when known.
    Ws
    Saturated value of W at the indicated temperature.
    RH
    Relative humidity, in percent.
    h
    Moist-air enthalpy (ET), in kJ/kg dry air.
    hf
    Enthalpy of liquid water or ice at the wet bulb temperature relative to liquid water at 0 °C, in kJ/kg water.
    v
    Specific volume (SV), in m³/kg dry air.
    Pv
    Water vapour partial pressure (PV), in kPa.
    Psat
    Saturation vapour pressure at the temperature in parentheses, in kPa.
    P
    Total atmospheric pressure (PT), in kPa.

    Quantity definitions and calculation range

    Worked example

    Relative Humidity 45.389339 % · Specific Volume 0.86427316 m³/kg dry air · height 0 m

    Wet Bulb Temperature = 18.72 °C
