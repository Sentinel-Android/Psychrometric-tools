    Why this pair is dependent

    At a given total pressure, these readings describe the same moisture content. The calculator gives the expected second reading for comparison. An independent reading such as dry bulb temperature is needed to determine a complete moist-air state.

    Psat(Tdp) = Pv

    Symbols

    Tdp
    Dew point temperature (DP), in °C.
    Pv
    Water vapour partial pressure (PV), in kPa.
    Psat
    Saturation vapour pressure at the temperature in parentheses, in kPa.
