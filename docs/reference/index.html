Psychrometric quantities and formulas

Definitions, units, and the moist-air model used by the calculators.

Quantity definitions

QuantityReport notationSI unitDefinition

Model and calculation range

The desktop Psychrometric Tools project used a standard-atmosphere pressure approximation and moist-air relationships for an Atmospheric Water Generator (AWG). The web calculator uses the project's pressure, enthalpy and volume constants, continuous saturation-pressure equations, and a wet bulb energy balance.

Enter dry bulb temperatures from −60 to 160 °C, wet bulb and dew point temperatures from −100 to 160 °C, and altitudes from −500 to 11,000 m, or supply a positive total pressure directly. Valid moist-air states have vapour pressure below total pressure and at or below saturation. Very dry air can have a dew point below the calculation range.

Moist-air formulas

q = W / (1 + W)
W = 0.622 × Pv / (P − Pv)
h = 1.005 Tdb + W (2500 + 1.88 Tdb)
v = 0.2871 (Tdb + 273.15)(1 + W/0.622) / P
RH = 100 × Pv / Psat(Tdb)
Pv = P W / (0.622 + W)
Psat(Tdp) = Pv
h(Tdb, W) + (Ws(Twb) − W) hf(Twb) = h(Twb, Ws(Twb))
W = q / (1 − q)
hf(Twb) = 4.186 Twb for Twb ≥ 0 °C
hf(Twb) = −333.6 + 2.1 Twb for Twb < 0 °C
Ws(Twb) = 0.622 Psat(Twb) / [P − Psat(Twb)]

Symbols

Tdb
Dry bulb temperature (DB), in °C.
Twb
Wet bulb temperature (WB), in °C.
Tdp
Dew point temperature (DP), in °C.
q
Specific humidity (SH), in kg water/kg moist air.
W
Intermediate water-vapour mass per kilogram of dry air, obtained from q/(1 − q) when known.
Ws
Saturated value of W at the indicated temperature.
RH
Relative humidity, in percent.
h
Moist-air enthalpy (ET), in kJ/kg dry air.
hf
Enthalpy of liquid water or ice at the wet bulb temperature relative to liquid water at 0 °C, in kJ/kg water.
v
Specific volume (SV), in m³/kg dry air.
Pv
Water vapour partial pressure (PV), in kPa.
Psat
Saturation vapour pressure at the temperature in parentheses, in kPa.
P
Total atmospheric pressure (PT), in kPa.

For overlap near freezing, the wet bulb calculation uses the liquid branch when available.
