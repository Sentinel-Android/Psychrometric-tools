Enthalpy

No calculations saved on this page yet.

    Input calculation

    Obtain the moisture term from the first reading, then calculate the dry bulb temperature from the second property.

    W = q / (1 − q)
    Tdb = P × v / [0.2871 (1 + W/0.622)] − 273.15

    Formula for enthalpy

    Sensible and latent energy of moist air per kilogram of dry air, relative to the model’s reference state.

    h = 1.005 Tdb + W (2500 + 1.88 Tdb)

    Symbols

    Tdb
    Dry bulb temperature (DB), in °C.
    q
    Specific humidity (SH), in kg water/kg moist air.
    W
    Intermediate water-vapour mass per kilogram of dry air, obtained from q/(1 − q) when known.
    h
    Moist-air enthalpy (ET), in kJ/kg dry air.
    v
    Specific volume (SV), in m³/kg dry air.
    P
    Total atmospheric pressure (PT), in kPa.

    Quantity definitions and calculation range

    Worked example

    Specific Humidity 0.01 kg water/kg moist air · Specific Volume 0.86427316 m³/kg dry air · height 0 m

    Enthalpy = 52.90 kJ/kg dry air
