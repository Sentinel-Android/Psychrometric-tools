    Input calculation

    Obtain the moisture term from the first reading, then calculate the dry bulb temperature from the second property.

    W = q / (1 − q)
    Tdb = (h − 2500 W) / (1.005 + 1.88 W)

    Formula for relative humidity

    Water vapour partial pressure expressed as a percentage of saturation pressure at the dry bulb temperature.

    RH = 100 × Pv / Psat(Tdb)

    Symbols

    Tdb
    Dry bulb temperature (DB), in °C.
    q
    Specific humidity (SH), in kg water/kg moist air.
    W
    Intermediate water-vapour mass per kilogram of dry air, obtained from q/(1 − q) when known.
    RH
    Relative humidity, in percent.
    h
    Moist-air enthalpy (ET), in kJ/kg dry air.
    Pv
    Water vapour partial pressure (PV), in kPa.
    Psat
    Saturation vapour pressure at the temperature in parentheses, in kPa.

    Quantity definitions and calculation range

    Worked example

    Specific Humidity 0.01 kg water/kg moist air · Enthalpy 52.900253 kJ/kg dry air · height 0 m

    Relative Humidity = 45.39 %
