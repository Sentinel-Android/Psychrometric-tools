    Input calculation

    Apply the wet bulb energy balance to find W and then use the enthalpy to recover the dry bulb temperature. Near 0 °C, small input errors can cause a large change in the result.

    W = Ws + [h − h(Twb, Ws)] / hf(Twb)
    Tdb = (h − 2500 W) / (1.005 + 1.88 W)

    At exactly 0 °C the liquid-water reference gives zero for the enthalpy of the added water. Use a different known property to determine the state.

    Symbols

    Tdb
    Dry bulb temperature (DB), in °C.
    Twb
    Wet bulb temperature (WB), in °C.
    W
    Intermediate water-vapour mass per kilogram of dry air, obtained from q/(1 − q) when known.
    Ws
    Saturated value of W at the indicated temperature.
    h
    Moist-air enthalpy (ET), in kJ/kg dry air.
    hf
    Enthalpy of liquid water or ice at the wet bulb temperature relative to liquid water at 0 °C, in kJ/kg water.
