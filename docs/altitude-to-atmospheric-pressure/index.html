    Conversion formulas

    P = 101.325 × (1 − 2.25577 × 10−5 × z)5.2559
    z = [1 − (P / 101.325)1 / 5.2559] / (2.25577 × 10−5)

    Symbols

    P
    Total atmospheric pressure (PT), in kPa.
    z
    Height above mean sea level, in metres.

    101.325 kPa is the standard sea-level pressure. Use altitudes from −500 to 11,000 m. Actual barometric pressure varies with weather.
