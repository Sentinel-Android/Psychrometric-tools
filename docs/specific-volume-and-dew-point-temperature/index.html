    Input calculation

    Obtain the moisture term from the first reading, then calculate the dry bulb temperature from the second property.

    Pv = Psat(Tdp)
    W = 0.622 × Pv / (P − Pv)
    Tdb = P × v / [0.2871 (1 + W/0.622)] − 273.15

    Symbols

    Tdb
    Dry bulb temperature (DB), in °C.
    Tdp
    Dew point temperature (DP), in °C.
    W
    Intermediate water-vapour mass per kilogram of dry air, obtained from q/(1 − q) when known.
    v
    Specific volume (SV), in m³/kg dry air.
    Pv
    Water vapour partial pressure (PV), in kPa.
    Psat
    Saturation vapour pressure at the temperature in parentheses, in kPa.
    P
    Total atmospheric pressure (PT), in kPa.
